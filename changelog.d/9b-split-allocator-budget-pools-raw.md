Slice 2 of 3 of the 9b size-outlier split of `allocator/mod.rs` (slice 1, #209, moved memory-type selection and defragmentation planning). Statistics, budget queries and pressure callbacks on `Allocator` move to `allocator/budget.rs`, custom pools to `allocator/pools.rs`, and the raw `vkAllocateMemory`/`vkMapMemory` calls with the dedicated-allocation path to `allocator/raw.rs`. The four raw-call functions become `pub(super)` (they are called across the new files); no public item changes. A move, not a rewrite: the cherry-picks of the old `day-2026-11-26-9b` branch (39fd4f6, b2eb197) re-cut onto current main. The private pass-through `Allocator::pick_memory_type` stays in `mod.rs`: inlining it is the MOD-008 item of slice 3. Evidence is in the PR body.
