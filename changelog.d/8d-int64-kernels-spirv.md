kind: SPIR-V kernels lower I64 and U64 compute dtypes (ROADMAP 8d, the road to 64-bit atomics and 8h)
---
`vulkane-spirv-emit`'s `lower_kernel` now lowers kernels at `I64` and `U64`: 64-bit buffers (stride 8), locals, add/sub/mul, the ruled total `Div`/`Rem` (zero divisor gives the dividend, `MIN / -1 = MIN`, `Rem` as the `Select` composition), shifts masked mod 64 and the arithmetic/logical `>>` by signedness. The `Int64` capability and 64-bit types are declared only for such a kernel: every 32-bit kernel lowers to byte-identical SPIR-V (golden test over seven kernels written before the change). `ExecId` is a `U32` in the IR, so a 64-bit kernel reaches its thread index through a new integer `Cast` (wrapping, to the compute dtype only; float and clamping casts still decline). Plan lowering (`Backend::lower`/`supports_dtype`) still declines 64-bit integers; F32/F64 kernels still decline ("integer kernels only"); a 64-bit atomic is a typed decline naming `Int64Atomics` until its slice. The host must enable `shaderInt64`; the emitter does not see the device. Tests: nine results per thread across the 32-bit boundary at I64 and U64 on a device (shaderInt64=true on the harness GPU), a host oracle at 32 bits that differs in every column (the truncating-lowering mutant), module checks under spirv-val.
